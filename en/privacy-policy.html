Privacy Policy

At Raven3, we value the privacy of everyone who visits our site, contacts us, or hires our services. This policy explains what information we may collect, how we use it, which tools we work with, and what rights you have over your data.

Acceptance of this policy

Accessing and using the raven3.com.ar website implies acknowledgment of this Privacy Policy.

By browsing the site, completing forms, subscribing to the newsletter, or contacting Raven3 through any of its channels, you agree that we may process your information in accordance with this policy and applicable law.

What data we may collect

1. Data you provide directly

  • Name.
  • Email address.
  • Phone number, if you choose to share it.
  • Company, brand, or project name.
  • Messages or inquiries submitted through forms.
  • Any other information you voluntarily share with us by email, forms, or WhatsApp.

2. Technical and browsing data

  • IP address.
  • Browser and device type.
  • Operating system.
  • Pages visited and time spent browsing.
  • Interaction events within the site.
  • Technical data derived from cookies, tags, logs, or measurement tools.

How we use your information

We may use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to commercial or technical inquiries.
  • Prepare proposals, quotes, or diagnostics.
  • Manage our relationship with clients and prospective clients.
  • Send updates, content, or communications you requested.
  • Measure traffic, behavior, and site performance.
  • Improve user experience, security, and performance.
  • Prevent abuse, spam, fraud, or unauthorized use.
  • Comply with legal or tax obligations where applicable.

Cookies, tags, and analytics

This site may use first-party and third-party cookies, as well as similar technologies, to understand how the site is used, improve navigation, and measure results.

1. What they may record

  • Sessions and browsing preferences.
  • Page visits and on-site events.
  • Aggregated information about devices and behavior.

2. Analytics tools

Raven3 may use analytics and measurement tools such as Google Analytics to obtain usage, performance, and aggregated behavioral statistics for the site.

3. Managing cookies

You can configure your browser to reject, limit, or delete cookies. Keep in mind that some site features may be affected if you disable certain cookies or scripts.

Forms, inquiries, and contact channels

When you complete a form on the site or contact Raven3, the information submitted may be processed through third-party tools specifically designed to receive and manage messages.

  • We use the data exclusively to respond to inquiries, evaluate projects, follow up commercially, or maintain the communication you requested.
  • The site may include anti-spam or verification mechanisms, such as reCAPTCHA or other protection solutions.
  • If you initiate contact via WhatsApp, that interaction is also subject to that platform's own policies and terms.

Newsletter and subscriptions

If you subscribe to Raven3's newsletter, we may store your email address and the minimum technical information necessary to manage the subscription, avoid duplicates, validate delivery, and maintain the subscriber list.

  • The content we send may include updates, technical insights, articles, launch announcements, or communications related to web development, performance, and technical SEO.
  • You may request to unsubscribe or delete your email by writing to hola@raven3.com.ar.

Third-party services and external processing

To operate properly, the Raven3 website or its services may rely on external providers for infrastructure, forms, analytics, security, automation, email, or communication.

These may include services such as:

  • Google Analytics or equivalent measurement services.
  • Formspree or similar form-handling tools.
  • Google reCAPTCHA or other anti-spam systems.
  • Hosting, CDN, DNS, cloud, or edge function providers.
  • WhatsApp for user-initiated contact.

These third parties may process certain information in accordance with their own privacy policies and terms of service.

Data retention period

We will retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy, maintain the business relationship, respond to requests, resolve issues, or comply with legal, contractual, or tax obligations.

Once the data is no longer needed, it may be deleted, anonymized, or blocked, as appropriate.

Security and confidentiality

Raven3 adopts reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, alteration, or improper disclosure.

  • Use of secure connections where applicable.
  • Restricted access to sensitive information.
  • Sound technical and operational safeguarding practices.

Even so, no system that operates over the Internet can guarantee absolute security. While we work to reduce risk, we cannot ensure total invulnerability against external incidents, attacks, or third-party failures.

Your rights over your data

Regarding your personal data, and to the extent applicable, you may request:

  • Access to the information we hold about you.
  • Updates or corrections.
  • Deletion or erasure.
  • Withdrawal of consent for certain uses.

To exercise any of these rights, write to us at hola@raven3.com.ar.

Minors

Raven3's services and the site's commercial inquiries are intended for individuals with sufficient legal capacity to enter into contracts under applicable law.

If we become aware that personal data has been submitted by a minor without appropriate involvement from their legal guardians, we may delete that information when appropriate.

Changes to this policy

Raven3 may update this Privacy Policy to reflect legal, technical, operational, or service-related changes.

The version currently in effect will always be the one published on this page. We recommend reviewing it periodically.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or about how we handle your information, you can write to hola@raven3.com.ar.

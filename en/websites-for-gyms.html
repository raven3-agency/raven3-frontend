WEBSITES FOR GYMS

Turn online visitors into real sign-ups

Websites for gyms, boxes and training centers that want to showcase plans, schedules, classes and make it easy to join.

I want a website for my gym View projects
THE PROBLEM

Prospects compare gyms before they sign up

They need to see classes, schedules, location, plans, atmosphere and ways to get in touch. If your gym doesn't have a website, the competitor that does gets the sign-up.

  • Your gym doesn't show up in search results. Whoever has their own website ranks first and gets the new member.

  • Instagram shows activity but doesn't organize plans, schedules, classes or pricing. Prospects can't find what they need to decide.

  • Google Business gives basic info but doesn't show your real value: trainers, atmosphere, training styles or brand identity.

  • You get the same WhatsApp questions over and over about schedules, prices and classes because there's no single place with all the info.

THE SOLUTION

Everything your prospect needs to sign up

A website that centralizes plans, schedules, classes, atmosphere and direct contact to turn curious visitors into members.

  • Plans and pricing laid out clearly so prospects can compare options and decide without having to ask.

  • Visible schedules and classes with a clear grid so members know exactly when and what they can train.

  • A gallery of the space that captures the energy of the place: equipment, layout and community.

  • A sign-up form so prospects can leave their details and start the process without delay.

  • Direct WhatsApp for questions so members don't have to go hunting for your number.

FEATURES

Everything your fitness website includes

Every feature is designed so prospects find exactly what they're looking for and sign up: plans, classes, schedules and contact.

Plans and pricing

Weight training, group classes, day passes and special plans with clear, comparable pricing.

Schedules

An up-to-date schedule grid with time slots, classes and available days for each activity.

Classes

A description of every class or training style: CrossFit, functional, spinning, yoga, boxing and more.

Trainers

Team profiles with specialty, experience and photo to build trust before the first class.

Gallery

Photos of the space, equipment, classes in action and community that capture the energy of the place.

WhatsApp

A direct contact button for quick questions, free trials or sign-ups via WhatsApp.

Sign-up form

A form for prospects to leave their details, choose a plan and start the sign-up process online.

Location

An embedded map with exact address and directions so members find you with no trouble.

Promotions

A section for active promotions: free trial class, sign-up discounts, 2-for-1 deals and seasonal offers.

Local SEO

Optimization so your gym shows up on Google when people search "gym near me" in your area.

BENEFITS

What changes when your gym has its own website

It's not just about being online. It's about controlling how people see you, what information they get, and how they end up signing up.

More sign-ups

Prospects who arrive ready to commit because they already saw plans, schedules, classes and atmosphere before contacting you.

Fewer repetitive questions

All the info is always available: pricing, schedules, classes and location, without answering the same question one by one.

Professionalism

Your website reflects the quality of your gym. A polished presentation builds trust and sets you apart.

Presence on Google

You show up when someone searches "gym in [your area]." Without a website, that traffic goes straight to your competitors.

The problem

Why can a site fail to rank even with great content?

  • Slow or bloated pages.
  • Indexation or crawlability issues.
  • Disorganized internal architecture.
  • Weak semantic hierarchy.
The solution

Technical optimization built around crawlability, performance and structure

Ideal for: Businesses, brands and projects that already have a live site but suspect their technical structure is holding back growth.

What we review in a technical SEO audit

Crawlability and indexation

Review of indexable pages, blocks, technical status codes and signals that affect how Google discovers the site.

Core Web Vitals and performance

Evaluation of load time, resource weight, images, scripts, rendering and other critical experience factors.

Internal architecture

Analysis of page hierarchy, internal linking, depth and overall site organization.

Semantics and HTML structure

Review of headings, semantic usage, markup and structural consistency across key pages.

Metadata and critical elements

Titles, descriptions, canonicals, images, relevant attributes and other important technical signals.

Accessibility and experience

We identify issues affecting comprehension, navigation and experience quality on desktop and mobile.

Benefits of getting technical SEO right

  • Better crawling and indexation: Google can discover, process and interpret the site's key pages more effectively.
  • A stronger foundation to rank on: Content and authority perform better when technical structure isn't holding them back.
  • Stronger performance and UX: A faster, better-organized site improves experience, engagement and overall quality signals.
  • Technical scalability: The architecture is better prepared to support new pages, categories, services or integrations.

Why work on technical SEO with Raven3?

  • Real technical perspective: We analyze the site from a development, performance and structural standpoint — not just automated tooling.
  • Performance-first approach: Core Web Vitals, resource weight, rendering and load time are all part of the project's technical analysis.
  • Technical SEO applied to the business: We prioritize what actually moves the needle on visibility, experience and growth capacity.
  • Architecture built to scale: We aim for structural improvement, not one-off fixes, so the site grows on a cleaner foundation.

What kinds of sites benefit from technical SEO

  • Corporate sites: To improve semantic structure, speed, internal linking and indexation capacity.
  • E-commerce and catalogs: Where architecture, pagination, filters, categories and technical performance have a major SEO impact.
  • Sites with growing content: Blogs, resource hubs or sites with many URLs that need a clear structure to scale.
  • Projects with performance issues: Slow or heavy sites where technical optimization can improve experience and quality signals.

The technical criteria we prioritize

  • Semantic architecture and correct heading structure.
  • Image, resource and load optimization.
  • Script control and unnecessary weight reduction.
  • Core Web Vitals improvement.
  • Internal linking structure and depth.
  • Accessibility as part of technical quality.
  • A structural foundation built to grow.
  • Technical SEO isn't just tools: Tools help surface symptoms, but real improvement comes from understanding the site's architecture and applying changes with sound technical judgment.

How technical SEO relates to web development

  • Heavy templates that slow down page load.
  • HTML structures with weak semantics.
  • Reliance on unnecessary builders or plugins.
  • Architectures that are hard to scale or link well.
  • Technical issues that affect UX and crawlability.

Frequently asked questions about technical SEO

Common questions about this service and its real impact on a site.

What is technical SEO?

It's the work done on a site's technical structure to improve crawlability, indexation, speed, architecture and experience.

Does technical SEO improve rankings?

Yes. It doesn't replace content or authority, but it strengthens the foundation so Google can process the site more effectively.

What does a technical SEO audit include?

It can include a review of indexability, performance, Core Web Vitals, architecture, markup, metadata and other structural factors.

Does Raven3 also implement technical fixes?

Yes. Raven3's approach combines diagnosis with technical resolution, especially when the problem lives in the site's architecture.

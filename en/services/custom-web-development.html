Why isn't your website generating leads or sales?

Most websites are built on bloated templates and plugin-heavy systems. The result: slow sites, hard to maintain, with a weak technical foundation that Google penalizes in the rankings.

  • Poor performance and long load times.
  • Technical SEO and indexability issues.
  • Dependency on heavy plugins or page builders.
  • Difficulty scaling new features down the line.
  • Weaker user experience and lower conversion.

The real problem

A website shouldn't just look good. It also needs to load fast, be easy for Google to crawl, adapt well to mobile, and support the growth of the business without breaking.

Custom web development: the difference between having a presence and generating business

At Raven3 we build websites from scratch using modern technologies and a performance-first architecture. Every project is designed specifically around the client's business, prioritizing speed, SEO positioning, maintainability, and scalability.

Unlike a generic solution, a custom-built website lets you define exactly what the project needs, avoiding unnecessary code, artificial dependencies, and technical bottlenecks. That translates into a cleaner, faster foundation that's built to evolve.

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to common questions about custom web development.

What is a custom-built website?

It's a site built from scratch for a specific business, without generic templates or rigid structures.

How long does it take to build a website?

Depending on the project, timelines can range from 3 to 8 weeks, based on scope, content, validation, and integrations.

Why avoid templates?

They tend to add unnecessary weight, limit customization, and hurt speed, scalability, and technical SEO.

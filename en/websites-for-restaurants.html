THE PROBLEM

Customers search Google before they book a table

If your website doesn't show menu, ambience, hours and reservations clearly, you're losing customers who were already interested.

  • Your restaurant doesn't show up the way it should in search results. You lose visibility to competitors who already have their own web presence.

  • Instagram shows activity but doesn't organize the full experience: menu, location, hours and reservations end up scattered or incomplete.

  • Google Business gives basic information but doesn't build a brand. It doesn't convey your identity, your cooking or the atmosphere of your place.

  • You get the same WhatsApp questions over and over about hours, menu and location because there's no single place where all that information lives.

THE SOLUTION

Everything your customer needs, in one place

A website that centralizes your restaurant's entire experience: menu, reservations, photos, location and direct contact.

  • An updatable digital menu so your customers see dishes, prices and options before they arrive.

  • An integrated reservation system that lets customers book a table straight from the website, no phone calls needed.

  • A gallery that conveys the experience of the place: dishes, ambience, team and real moments.

  • Location, hours and contact always visible. Everything one tap away from a phone.

  • Direct WhatsApp for quick questions without customers having to hunt for your number.

FEATURES

Digital menu

Online reservations

An integrated booking system so customers pick date, time and party size without calling.

Direct WhatsApp

A quick-contact button for questions, orders or reservations straight through WhatsApp.

Google Maps

An integrated map with exact location, directions and a direct link so customers get there without trouble.

Hours

Opening days and hours always visible and up to date, so no one shows up to a closed door.

Photo gallery

Ambience / experience

Special events

A space to promote events, seasonal menus, special dates and news from your place.

Delivery links

Direct shortcuts to your delivery-app profiles for customers who'd rather order from home.

Mobile optimization

Designed for phones, where most of your customers are searching for a place to eat.

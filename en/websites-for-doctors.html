WEBSITES FOR DOCTORS

Your patients search Google before booking an appointment

A professional website for doctors, practices and specialists who want to organize their digital presence and make it easy for patients to get in touch.

I want a website for my practice
THE PROBLEM

Without a website, you depend on word of mouth

Patients search for specialists on Google before booking an appointment. A clear website helps explain your specialty, experience, location, insurance coverage, scheduling and care model. Without one, you depend on being found through referrals alone.

  • Patients can't find your specialty or address when they search on Google.

  • You get calls asking things that a website could answer: insurance plans, hours, directions.

  • Your Instagram profile doesn't convey the seriousness and trust that a healthcare professional needs.

  • You lose patients who have no easy way to reach you or book an appointment after hours.

THE SOLUTION

Your practice, visible and accessible

A complete professional profile. Clear specialties. Online or WhatsApp booking. Location and insurance coverage. A design that's serious, clear and accessible.

  • A complete professional profile with your specialty, background and photo.

  • Clear specialties so patients understand what you treat.

  • Online or WhatsApp booking available around the clock.

  • Location and accepted insurance plans visible without needing a phone call.

  • A serious, accessible design that conveys professional trust.

WHAT'S INCLUDED

Everything your online practice needs

Every section is designed to answer your patients' most common questions and cut down on repetitive calls.

Professional profile

Photo, name, license, background and education in a clear, trustworthy profile.

Specialties

A dedicated section for each specialty or treatment you offer, described clearly for the patient.

Appointments

Integration with an online booking system or a direct WhatsApp button to schedule.

Location

An interactive map, address and directions to the practice so patients have no doubts.

Insurance plans

A clear list of the insurance and health plans you accept. Patients know before they call.

FAQ

Answers to the most common questions: test preparation, costs, first-time visits.

Direct WhatsApp

A floating WhatsApp button with a pre-filled message so patients can reach you instantly.

Contact form

A professional form for inquiries, referrals or after-hours appointment requests.

Local SEO

Optimization so you show up in searches like "cardiologist near me" or "dermatologist in [your area]".

Accessible design

Legible typography, proper contrast and intuitive navigation for every kind of patient.

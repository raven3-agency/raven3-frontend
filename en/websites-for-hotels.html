THE PROBLEM

Relying only on platforms shrinks your brand and your margin

Booking, Airbnb and other platforms bring visibility, but they charge high commissions and the guest never gets to know your brand. A website of your own changes that.

  • Booking platforms charge commissions of 15% to 25% on every reservation. Your profitability drops with every guest who books through that channel.

  • Guests don't remember your brand — they remember the platform. Without your own site, you build no identity or direct relationship with the people who visit you.

  • There's nowhere to show the full experience: rooms, amenities, surroundings, nearby activities, and what makes your property unique.

  • Guests who book directly are more profitable, return more often, and recommend you better. Without a website, that channel simply doesn't exist.

THE SOLUTION

Your property, with its own presence and direct bookings

A website that showcases rooms, amenities, location, and lets guests book with no middleman.

  • Rooms with gallery and descriptions so guests can see every option before deciding.

  • Commission-free direct bookings through a form, WhatsApp, or an integrated booking engine.

  • Services and local experiences to convey what makes your property unique.

  • Location and nearby attractions with an integrated map so guests can plan their stay.

  • Direct WhatsApp for quick questions about availability, rates and conditions.

Local & tourism SEO

Optimization so your property shows up in searches like "hotel in [destination]" or "cabins in [area]".

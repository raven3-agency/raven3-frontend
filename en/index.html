SYS.09 // FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What our clients
ask before getting started

How much does a custom website cost?

A professional custom website starts at USD 800–1,500 for institutional projects and scales based on features, integrations and complexity. We put together clear proposals with defined scope before any work begins.

How long does it take to build a website?

A custom institutional site can be ready in 3 to 5 weeks. More complex projects like e-commerce or platforms with integrations take between 6 and 10 weeks, with a timeline defined from day one.

Why choose custom development over WordPress or templates?

A custom-built site loads faster, ranks better on Google, is more secure, and doesn't depend on plugins that break with every update. If you need real performance and solid technical SEO, custom development is the better investment.

What markets does Raven3 work with?

We work with clients worldwide. Communication is 100% remote and seamless, regardless of time zone.

What's included in the free audit?

A technical diagnostic of your current site: performance, Core Web Vitals, technical SEO, HTML structure and improvement opportunities. We send you a report with real findings and concrete recommendations, no strings attached.

What happens after the site is finished?

We handle deployment, set up analytics, monitor post-launch performance and offer ongoing technical support. Your site isn't abandoned once it ships.

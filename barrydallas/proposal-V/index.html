From Darkness into Light

Preceeding pages sequentially unfold:
the book's title then adds the author;
one raindrop becomes many “concentric waves”
in 2D plan erasing/eliminating the black
background;

This page converts concentric plans into 3D
spheres continuing to expand and erase/eliminate
the black background until it's white/blank;

adds author then title;
and begins again all black.
